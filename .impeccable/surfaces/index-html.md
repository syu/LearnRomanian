## Scope

`index.html`, the whole app (home, quiz, pairs, cards, results). Mode: Operate. Desktop first (laptop, mouse and keyboard); must still work on a phone.

## Audience and task

One Russian-speaking learner in Brașov drilling the vocabulary from their Romanian course in short daily sessions (5–15 min), mostly Микс. Success: start in one click, see the word big, hear it, answer, feel progress. Must keep: every function, emoji pictures, Russian UI, offline use. Must not be busy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every word is a primer page. The Romanian word owns the page at primer scale next to its emoji, and the teacher's stamp marks the answer. Refuses the category default: a grey flashcard centred on a blank screen with a green progress bar.

OWN-WORLD: Abecedar in deep teal and crayon pastels. Deep sea-teal rail (#0e4f55) with white text; mint-white page (#f4fbf9); ink #14262b. Crayon pastels as flat fills: lavender (letter tile), apricot, pistachio, lemon, lime. Brick-red teacher's stamp (#c8452c) for marks and mistakes; teal for correct. No pink. Rubik for words and headings (primer letterforms), Golos Text for UI. Rounded 14–28px tiles, flat colour, no shadows beyond a hairline.

STORY: Open → see topics on the teal rail and today's word on the page → press Начать · Микс → answer pages turn in the same frame → the stamp marks each answer → results end with a calificativ (FB / B / S / I) and the words to repeat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 300px teal rail: brand, topic checklist with counts and learned ticks, settings link at the bottom. Main page: header line (words to practise today, yesterday's stamp). Primer plate: lavender letter tile "Pp", word huge (≈120px Rubik 800), transcription and example, Russian meaning, emoji ≈160px. Bottom: large teal Начать · Микс button (primary action, Enter), other modes as pastel chips.

FORM: Abecedar (Romanian school primer), position 3 of 7 on the grounded list, seed key 656427c1, palette variant "Deep teal & crayons" with green/yellow pastels instead of pink (user steer). Signature move: the teacher's stamp: answers are marked with a rotated stamp (FB ✓ or ✗) that stays on the option; results give a Romanian school calificativ. Raises kept: one word owns the field; states stamp themselves; one scale and baseline for every question page; the frame never moves between questions; the focused option lifts, the rest quiet down.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
